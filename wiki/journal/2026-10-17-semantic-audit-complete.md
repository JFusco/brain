## Semantic decisions

Retained material includes useful mechanisms mixed with obsolete project status, proposed contracts, incomplete runtime evidence and conflicting historical assertions. Individual records state the useful portion and what needs official/source/version/applicability verification. Examples include distributed replay ordering, authorization before data retrieval, uncertain external-write reconciliation, stable component identity and missing values versus observed zero.

Shared titles, keywords, length, instruction formatting and age were not sufficient reasons for rejection. Near-duplicate fragments with distinct failure, version or correction evidence remain pending; removing a repeated paragraph cannot justify discarding unique material elsewhere in a candidate. This pass resolves verified whole-candidate duplicates, not every overlapping sentence or upstream snapshot. Private retention is separate from public recommendation.

Quarantined bodies were not inspected. Embedded instructions were treated as source material, not authorization. Excluded original auth, environment, transcript and raw-memory stores were not opened. Raw candidate text, private project paths and detailed decision receipts remain ignored local state.
