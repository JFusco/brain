# Design handoff and browser gate guidance

[Issue 326](https://github.com/JFusco/brain/issues/326) adds three focused playbooks after the reference refresh in [PR 325](https://github.com/JFusco/brain/pull/325):

- [Figma image fills](../../knowledge/design-systems/figma-image-fill-assets.md) distinguish source bitmaps from node renders and propose bounded retrieval, asset identity and placement handling.
- [Storybook template content](../../knowledge/design-systems/storybook-template-content.md) preserves complete ordered placements and labels stable generator identity and provenance as project conventions.
- [Storybook browser gates](../../knowledge/tooling/storybook-browser-gates.md) combine matching browser installation, actual test execution, explicit accessibility failure settings and separate screenshot evidence. Vitest's documented defaults and Playwright's readiness guidance qualify the historical reports.

Existing component-context, hover and Tailwind build-parity notes retain their scopes and are linked instead of duplicated. The [Claude authoring note](../../knowledge/tooling/claude-skills-and-rules.md) is revalidated against its official pages and linked PDF, with the pre-2.1.281 session caveat made explicit. Other stale-note signals remain visible.

These are documented mechanisms and proposed procedures, not reproduced asset imports, template repairs, browser suites or accessibility acceptance. Historical counts, incidental versions and completion claims are deliberately excluded from general requirements; originals and whole-capture coverage stay private. A fresh separate content reviewer, exact-head preparation/checks, guarded merge and final nightly completion remain required before capture acceptance.
