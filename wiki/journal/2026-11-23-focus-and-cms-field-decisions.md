# Focus and CMS field decisions

[Issue 445](https://github.com/JFusco/brain/issues/445) adds three bounded knowledge decisions after comparison with the existing accessibility and Contentstack hubs.

- [Forced-colors focus](../../knowledge/accessibility/forced-colors-keyboard-focus.md) explains why a shadow-only cue can disappear and proposes keyboard/visual verification in the user's palette.
- [Field visibility](../../knowledge/cms/contentstack/field-visibility-and-dependent-selectors.md) records current operand exclusions and per-rule limits without claiming a tested dependent-selector implementation.
- [Defaults and fallbacks](../../knowledge/cms/contentstack/defaults-and-site-fallbacks.md) separates native creation-time defaults from proposed site rendering policy, including explicit emptiness and authored-value preservation.

MDN, CSSWG and current Contentstack documentation ground the mechanisms. No browser test, CMS write, custom field or deployed fallback is claimed. Existing image, disclosure and editor-guidance notes cover distinct decisions and remain linked or unchanged.

Other stale signals and incomplete source research remain visible in private maintenance evidence. Fresh independent review of the committed content, native publication checks and successful final completion still precede capture acceptance.
