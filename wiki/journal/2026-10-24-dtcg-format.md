# Design Tokens format catalog

[Issue 191](https://github.com/JFusco/brain/issues/191) registers `dtcg-format`, a static HTML collection on `www.designtokens.org` limited to `/tr/2025.10/`. The 2025.10 index links its modules relatively, so the Format, Color and Resolver modules and the index itself are listed as additional pages with their published titles. Local `official sync` catalogued those four pages; the first sync had titled the index by its "Abstract" anchor, so its title was set explicitly.

The modules form the Final Community Group Report of 28 October 2025, published under the W3C Community Final Specification Agreement; they are not a W3C Standard. Editor's drafts are excluded. The Design systems hub now says its catalogs state their versions. The domain had no curated notes; the unscoped `component meaning Code Connect` lookup kept its Frontend note at position 1. See [official reference expansion](../topics/official-reference-expansion.md).
