# Design Tokens format catalog

[Issue 191](https://github.com/JFusco/brain/issues/191) registers `dtcg-format`, a static HTML collection on `www.designtokens.org` limited to `/tr/2025.10/`. The index's relative module links resolve, and an `#abstract` anchor link brings in the index itself. The Format, Color and Resolver modules and the index are also listed as additional pages so they carry their published titles; the first sync had titled the index "Abstract". Local `official sync` catalogued those four pages. The issue expected three module pages; the index cannot be excluded by prefix and is kept as the reports overview.

The modules form the Final Community Group Report of 28 October 2025, published under the W3C Community Final Specification Agreement; they are not a W3C Standard. Editor's drafts are excluded. The Design systems hub now says its catalogs state their versions. The domain had no curated notes; the unscoped `component meaning Code Connect` lookup kept its Frontend note at position 1.
