# GitHub-hosted official maintenance and verification

Joe requested maintenance and verification independent of the Mac/app. The [hosted procedure](../topics/hosted-maintenance.md) separates read-credential preparation/investigation, deterministic guarded publication and fresh completion-linked verification. Only official-source operational evidence/reference publication material is retained; private collection, inbox content, client terms, sessions, raw memories, keys and backups are excluded.

The explicit hosted profile reports unavailable cached historical bytes while requiring active, tracked and present historical integrity. Strict local defaults remain unchanged. The scanner is checksum-verified at its pinned version on the runner platform; the native privacy guard now resolves Node through the configured runner PATH.

The existing local jobs remain until actual hosted execution and verification pass; saved run/check results and any deployment repairs are recorded in the linked issue/PR. After handoff, private collection/curation remains local and official reference/link work is delegated. Configuration is not execution proof; manual proof is not a scheduled-run attestation, and a no-warning run leaves repair unexercised.

Rollback disables the hosted workflow and restores local reference ownership/publisher instructions without discarding private state or failure evidence. Existing historical snapshots and disabled backups are preserved. Deferred tests, fixtures, fresh-session tests and QA/review-agent audits were not added.
