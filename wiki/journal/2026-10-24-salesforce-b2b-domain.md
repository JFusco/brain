# Salesforce B2B Commerce knowledge domain

[Issue 177](https://github.com/JFusco/brain/issues/177) adds `commerce/salesforce-b2b` to the configured domains after `design-systems`, with a capture keyword entry after the design-system keywords. The nested hub routes LWR storefront, Experience Builder configuration and storefront customization questions, asks readers to keep platform release and template context, and links Frontend, Performance and Security. It starts with no curated notes and no official catalogs; no existing note covered Salesforce.

This completes the three domain changes of the source-gap plan. The README Coverage watch list names developer.salesforce.com, help.salesforce.com and github.com/forcedotcom as unregistered candidates. Two guessed Commerce guide URLs returned 404 while the Commerce developer center answered, so the registration must establish a retrievable official index first.
