# Daily Slack intake

The nightly claim points configured workers to a read-only provider preview.
Brain advances daily and seed coverage privately, retries independent gaps,
reconciles inbox receipts and requires current primary-source retrieval before
a technical decision can enter the inbox. Direct citations, applicability and
independent content review are required for publication.

The local roster is configured. Scheduled-worker preview, production capture,
publication and the two-week pilot review still require operational evidence.
Focused fixture scenarios cover old-thread proof, adaptive seeding, missed
dates, incomplete inventory, interruption, duplicate replay and zero lessons.
See the [worker procedure](../../docs/slack-intake.md).
